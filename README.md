We started by making a python script that could extract the captions, tables, footnotes and references of ten random htmls files that we downloaded on the first homework. After that, we proceeded to extract the claims.

-----
**How do we extract them?**

We took advantage of artificial intelligence models like chat gpt, claude and gemini to perform this task. In total, out of the ten files, they generated 27 files containing several claims in a txt format thanks to an ipynb script. Then, those files were converted to json thanks to another ipynb script. 
After this, we produced the groundtruth, by checking each file individually and closely to see if the extraction was as precise as possible.

-----
**Ok, and how do we know that what the AIs did was more or less accurate?**

For this purpose, we developed another python script that compared the claims generated by AI and the ones of the groundtruth. Whenever the similarity between two of them was higher than 65%, we considered it as a decent enough. So, to give a perspective about how accurate our AI generated claims are, the global precision, recall and f1 are: 0.84, 0.81 and 0.82 respectively.
